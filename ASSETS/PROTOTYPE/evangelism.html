The Battle Plan

Our operational timeline for Total Member Involvement (TMI).

01

Preparation

Jan - Mar 2026

Spiritual revival in all churches. Training of laity. Identification of Bible study interests. 40 Days of Prayer and Fasting.

02

Sowing

Apr - Jun 2026

Friendship evangelism. Medical missionary health expos. Distribution of GLOW tracts. Small group community service projects.

03

Reaping

Jul - Sep 2026

The "Loud Cry" campaigns. Simultaneous city-wide public evangelism. WAD Impact events. Baptismal harvesting.

04

Retention

Oct - Dec 2026

Discipleship masterclasses. Integration of new members into Small Care Groups. Nurturing and grounding in the faith.

Methods of Engagement

How we will reach the unreached.

Digital Army

Turning every member's phone into a pulpit. Utilizing WhatsApp status evangelism, Facebook live streams, and digital Bible studies to reach the youth.

Literature Evangelism

Scattering the truth like leaves of autumn. Massive distribution of "The Great Controversy" and health literature in marketplaces and schools.

Medical Missionary

The right arm of the gospel. Free medical checkups, eye screenings, and health lectures to break down prejudice and open hearts.

Small Group Evangelism

Turning homes into centers of light. Neighbors inviting neighbors for Bible study and fellowship in a non-threatening environment.
