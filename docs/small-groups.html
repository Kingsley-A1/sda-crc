Why Small Care Groups?

The West-Central Africa Division (WAD) has mandated that the church must move beyond Sabbath worship into daily fellowship. Small Care Groups are the engine of Total Member Involvement (TMI).

A Small Care Group is a weekly gathering of 6-12 people for Bible study, prayer, and relational evangelism. It is where:

  • Needs are met through mutual care.
  • Spiritual gifts are discovered and used.
  • New believers are nurtured and retained.
  • The "Church" becomes accessible to the community.
