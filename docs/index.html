Small Care Groups

Connect deeply with fellow believers in intimate settings for spiritual growth and mutual support.

Our Small Care Groups provide a nurturing environment where members can share their faith journeys, pray together, and support one another through life's challenges. These groups meet regularly in homes or local venues, fostering meaningful relationships and spiritual development.

Whether you're seeking deeper fellowship, Bible study, or simply a place to belong, our care groups welcome everyone. Join a group near you and experience the power of community in Christ.

Adventist Oriented Evangelism

Sharing the everlasting gospel through Christ-centered outreach and community service.

Our evangelism efforts are rooted in the Adventist message of hope, focusing on proclaiming the three angels' messages and preparing people for Christ's soon return. Through various programs including public evangelistic series, literature distribution, and community service projects, we reach out to our neighbors with love and compassion.

Get involved in spreading the gospel through our evangelistic initiatives, from door-to-door outreach to digital ministry and health expos. Together, we can make a difference in our communities and hasten the coming of our Lord.
