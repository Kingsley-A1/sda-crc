Adventist Youth Ministries

Salvation and Service. Leading young people into a saving relationship with Jesus Christ and training them for service.

Our Mission

The Adventist Youth Ministries (AYM) of the Cross River Conference is dedicated to the salvation of youth and their training for service. We believe that the youth are not just the church of tomorrow, but the vibrant church of today.

Our ministry encompasses Adventurers (ages 4-9), Pathfinders (ages 10-15), and Senior Youth/Ambassadors (ages 16-21+). Through Bible study, community outreach, camping, and leadership training, we aim to produce a generation of young people who are ready to say, "I Will Go."

"Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity."
1 Timothy 4:12 (KJV)
